theme.tres

Godot 4.x. Put theme.tres in your project, then select a Control and set Theme (or Project Settings, GUI, Theme, Custom). A StyleBoxFlat can't blur what's behind it, so the .tres gives you the translucent glass look. For real backdrop blur, add the .gdshader as a ShaderMaterial on a Panel and set Shadow to 0.
